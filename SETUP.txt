IMPORTANT:
The current booking storage is browser-local for demonstration. It is NOT yet a shared online database.
To make bookings available to you from any phone/computer, connect Firebase/Supabase or another backend.
To activate real online payments, connect a payment provider (for Kenya, options include M-Pesa/card providers).
To enable automatic driver notifications, connect the database to WhatsApp/SMS/email/push notifications.
Google Maps can be connected for live pickup/destination and route distance.
